Your connections

Account connections are managed by the website operator. Your passwords and API credentials never belong in a booking form.

What happens at payment?

Your prepared Shohoz session opens inside SeatWatch. You control the provider page to choose a payment method and complete payment. SeatWatch never automatically submits a payment.

If Shohoz asks for an OTP or another verification, you complete that step yourself. A booking is only marked confirmed after the provider supplies a confirmation reference.
